Attendance Register Policy & Procedures
1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy establishes the rules and procedures governing the recording of student attendance through the QR‑based digital register system.
1.2 The objective is to ensure accuracy, integrity, and fairness in attendance tracking across all participating campuses.

2. Scope
2.1 This Policy applies to all students, faculty, and administrators utilizing the attendance register system.
2.2 Enforcement of disciplinary measures shall be subject to campus‑specific regulations.

3. Definitions
3.1 Valid Excuse shall mean a documented or verifiable reason for lateness or absence, including but not limited to medical certificates or official transport disruptions.
3.2 Late shall mean arrival sixteen (16) minutes or more after the scheduled commencement of class.
3.3 Fraudulent Login shall mean any attempt to mark attendance using another student’s credentials or while not physically present on campus.
3.4 Attendance Threshold shall mean the minimum requirement of eighty percent (80%) attendance to qualify for examination participation.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
4.1 Students shall:
a) Scan the QR code provided in class to access the attendance site;
b) Log in to mark presence using their student number and a randomly generated daily passcode;
c) Access their dashboard using email and password credentials, as determined by campus rules;
d) Maintain confidentiality of all login credentials.

4.2 Faculty shall:
a) Generate and distribute daily passcodes;
b) Monitor attendance records and override entries where appropriate;
c) Mark students absent if late beyond fifteen (15) minutes without a valid excuse;
d) Correct fraudulent or mistaken entries.

4.3 Administrators shall:
a) Assign system roles to individuals;
b) Ensure compliance with this Policy and system integrity;
c) Oversee archival and retention of attendance records.

5. Authentication and Access Control
5.1 Attendance login shall require a student number and a daily random passcode generated via SQL function.
5.2 Dashboard login shall require email and password credentials, with the type of email and password determined by campus rules.
5.3 System access shall be restricted to approved public IP ranges.
5.4 Audit logs shall record login attempts, timestamps, and IP addresses.
5.5 All data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest.

6. Attendance Rules
6.1 Students arriving sixteen (16) minutes or more late shall be marked absent unless a valid excuse is approved by faculty.
6.2 Household inconveniences, including but not limited to water outages, shall not constitute valid excuses.
6.3 Students marked present but not physically on campus shall be corrected to absent.
6.4 Credential sharing or fraudulent login attempts shall constitute misconduct subject to disciplinary review.
6.5 Faculty may override attendance status upon receipt of valid documentation.

7. Data Integrity and Security
7.1 Attendance logs shall be immutable for students.
7.2 Faculty and administrators may update records only for legitimate corrections.
7.3 Supabase backend shall enforce row‑level security; Firebase frontend shall be restricted by IP.
7.4 Daily passcode rotation shall prevent credential reuse or sharing.

8. Reporting and Notifications
8.1 Students shall have access to attendance summaries via their dashboard.
8.2 Faculty shall receive daily class attendance reports.
8.3 Administrators shall generate campus‑level compliance reports.
8.4 Parents or guardians shall be notified of low attendance below eighty percent (80%).

9. Archival and Retention
9.1 At the conclusion of each academic year, or upon student departure, a summary of attendance shall be created.
9.2 Records shall be archived in accordance with campus rules and retained for extended periods, typically decades, to support graduate verification.
9.3 Archived records shall be immutable and accessible only to authorized administrators.

10. Enforcement and Penalties
10.1 Penalties for lateness, absence, or fraudulent login shall be determined by campus‑specific regulations.
10.2 Faculty may deny examination access to students whose attendance falls below eighty percent (80%).
10.3 Repeated violations may be escalated to disciplinary committees.
10.4 Fraudulent login attempts may result in suspension or expulsion, subject to severity and campus rules.

11. Review and Amendment
11.1 This Policy shall be reviewed annually or upon material changes to the system.
11.2 Amendments shall be communicated to all stakeholders prior to enforcement.
11.3 Campus administrators may adapt enforcement details to comply with local regulations.
